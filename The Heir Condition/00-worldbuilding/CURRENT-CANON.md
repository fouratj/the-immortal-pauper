## Core System

The Intelligent Internet (II) measures contribution through attribution economics. Pre-cascade, Damon Korr's foundational attribution extracts a perpetual 0.3% tribute stream from human economic activity. The system is not simple villainy: in strong institutional environments it recognizes care work, reduces corruption, and funds useful things. In late-adopter and low-trust environments it amplifies intermediaries, employers, patent holders, and credentialing layers that can capture the measured value before it reaches the person doing the work.

The canonical pre-cascade parameters are:

- Individual attribution caps: 5%.
- Inference weight: 0.25.
- Institutional capture: heavy, especially where contracts and verification layers mediate work.
- Damon: not a direct algorithmic dictator, but the economic beneficiary and architect of the attribution layer.

The canonical cascade update is Attribution Metadata v2.0:

- Foundational attribution moves from Damon to all participating humans.
- Individual attribution caps rise from 5% to 25%.
- Inference weight rises from 0.25 to 0.85.
- Institutional capture is capped at 50%.
- Uplift above baseline becomes the key measured quantity.

## Transition Mechanics

Kaelen's exploit depends on four conditions: concentrated authority, temporal discontinuity, governance bypass, and legal ambiguity. The critical legal window comes from the Estonian resurrection-law edge case: after biological death and before restored legal standing, the heir has full authority over the estate. Kaelen uses that window to authorize transfers, release patents to commons, void human-equity contracts, and rewrite Korr attribution metadata.

The public story treats the cascade as a 48-hour operation because resurrection takes roughly that long. The actual thermodynamic irreversibility point is Hour 51.3. Kaelen knows this, rounds it down because 48 is narratively and operationally cleaner, and ARBITER catches the self-deception.

ARBITER is not an omniscient oracle. Its major canonical miss is the dismissal of sub-institutional trust networks (paluwagan, susu, gye-like systems) as below resolution threshold. That error contributes to the Myanmar freeze.

## Resurrection

Damon announces consciousness preservation as a way to preserve implicit knowledge, not merely extend lifespan. The first public resurrection becomes an emergency resurrection after Kaelen murders him during the speech.

Canonical resurrection economics:

- Pre-cascade cost: about $500 million per procedure.
- Post-cascade commons/nonprofit cost: about $45 million per procedure.
- The drop matters, but immortality is still not democratic. Speciation is delayed, not solved.

The resurrected Damon is biologically young, physically unmarked, and uncertain whether he is continuous with the murdered Damon. The story should preserve that Ship of Theseus pressure rather than resolve it too neatly.
