# Current Plot Structure

Source of truth: `../04-story`.

Last reconciled: 2026-06-22.

## Shape

The current manuscript is no longer the old five-document documentary frame. It is a direct chaptered structure with Act 0 grounding, Act 1 murder/activation, Act 2 diagnosis and planning, and Act 3 cascade/reckoning.

The current structural principle is not "the victim investigates the perfect crime." It is closer to:

**A systems murder whose consequences become visible faster than its moral meaning can settle.**

Kaelen drives the action, but the story keeps returning to the people whose lives are caught between financial speed and physical speed: Seo-jin, Rosa, Marco, Javi, Carlos, Margaret, Myanmar.

## Active Structural Problems

- The cascade is still split across three numbered files and can feel like repeated bunker authorization cycles. The intended next revision is a cleaner three-register structure: bunker, Manila, Seoul.
- Margaret Kowalski is referenced as a face for the futures-pricing gap but does not yet have her own chapter.
- Javi has only a small coda. He needs an ending that shows who he becomes when human-equity extraction collapses.
- Seo-jin's science survives through commons funding, but the loss of her consortium/team needs sharper treatment if that is the intended cost.
- The resurrection chapter currently reads the sentiment spike too cleanly. The intended revision is a fragmented readout: destruction and construction at once, with the payoff unresolved at Hour 48.
- The Confrontation has the right Myanmar argument, but should keep resisting vindication. Damon and Kaelen can both be right, which is worse than either of them winning.
