# Manuscript Evaluation — August 12, 2026

13 chapters · ~66,000 words · 4 independent graders, one fixed rubric. Every score is backed by a quoted passage from the manuscript. 5 = exemplary, 3 = serviceable, 1 = broken.

## Method

Each chapter was read in full and scored 1–5 on seven axes derived from the project's own style rules (`narrative-style.mdc`) and the known weaknesses in the session notes:

| Axis | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **P** Propulsion | Does the chapter move under its own power? Scene economy, pacing. |
| **C** Compression | "Say it, don't show it" — arch statement over blow-by-blow. Penalizes bloat and redundant beats. |
| **R** Register | Emotional range. Penalizes single-note interiority and repeating emotional cycles. |
| **L** Ledger balance | Are costs as vivid as benefits? Could the reader argue either side? |
| **S** Systems integrity | Internal consistency — clocks, prices, ages, who-knows-what, hard-to-vary cause-and-effect. |
| **V** Voice | Stephenson markers done well — knowing parentheticals, load-bearing coinages, digressions that snap back. |
| **T** Reader trust | No telegraphing, no narrator-explains, unglossed dialogue where the pattern is established. |

The rubric is repeatable: after each revision pass, re-grade the touched chapters against the same axes and watch the scores move. That is the whole point — the scores tell you whether a pass worked, so you never have to reread 400 pages to know where you stand.

## The Diagnosis in One Sentence

The prose is not the problem — Voice averages 4.2 and two chapters are publishable-grade. The manuscript's disease is its **numeric spine** (clocks, ages, prices, and counts contradict each other in six chapters) and a **narrator who decodes scenes the reader already understood**. Both are fixable mechanically, without new inspiration.

## Continuity Bugs Found (new — not in the TODO)

Severity: **Critical** = breaks plot logic a careful reader will catch · **Major** = breaks the numeric spine · **Minor** = names, arithmetic, typos.

### Critical

1. **The announcement year (05 → 05.1).** The Discontinuity's "in five weeks" (line ~121) and undated "April 15th" (line ~184) place the announcement in April 2049 — but execution is April 2050 and the Section 47.3 exploit closes "within days or weeks" of the announcement. As written, the door shuts a year before Kaelen walks through it. Date the announcement April 15, 2050 and audit every window reference against the thirteen-month gap.
2. **The signature paradox (07 → 08).** Ch 08's climax rests on Damon's signature validating the Myanmar override ("The biometric pad flashed green. The cascade completed.") — three days after Ch 07 spent forty lines establishing his signatures are void ("your legal standing is... undefined"). **Opportunity:** weaponize it — the override system accepts a dead man's biometrics while the law calls him dead, forcing the question of *whose signature this is*. Repairs the contradiction AND gives Ship of Theseus its load-bearing scene.
3. **Two timelines coexist manuscript-wide.** The 13-month decision→execution gap vs "three years" (of ARBITER mapping, of work, since Tanaka's interview) appearing in both Fifteen Moves and The Confrontation. Not a stray typo — systematic. Pick one and propagate.
4. **Cascade clock anchors are mutually incompatible (06–06.2).** Hour 6 = 6:17 AM Manila (Javi) *and* 06:17 KST (Seo-jin) — an hour apart. Rosa's Hour 21 scene is "five in the morning" (should be ~9 PM). Hour 30 narrates Hour 36 events (power surge, "thirty-six hours into dismantling"). Bangalore goes dark at Hour 38 (06.1) and again at Hour 41 (06.2).

### Major

5. **Resurrection access, 480× gap (06).** "Perhaps a hundred thousand" (line ~424) vs "forty-eight million people who could now afford immortality" (line ~428) — four lines apart, in the chapter's central moral calculation.
6. **Kaelen's age has three incompatible tracks.** Mother's death Sept 2037 at age 12 (→ 24 in 2049, internally consistent with the 2038/2040 memories); Bangalore's 27/28 (which also disagrees with itself within a week); Fifteen Moves V's "fourteen, three Christmases ago" (→ born ~2032). Pick the 2037 track and propagate. Also "eighteen years later" (Discontinuity ~269) should be ~twelve.
7. **The surveillance contradiction (06.1–06.2).** Kaelen "decided early not to surface individual data" yet watches Margaret's face and Rosa's kitchen in real time — through CarePath channels his own cascade terminated at Hour 35.
8. **Soon-ok's age math (00).** Stated "eighty-nine" but her own dates (seven in Busan 1951; twenty-three at Guro 1967) make her ~106; her brother is two and four years old in the same winter (lines ~28 vs ~54).
9. **Equity math (01).** "His 15% of a ₱4M baseline was ₱600K on paper" contradicts the uplift-from-baseline contract structure the chapter itself establishes (paper value at baseline = zero). The countdown also runs backwards (11 hours remaining in a scene placed before a 21-hours-remaining scene). Rico says "eighteen percent, just like you" minutes after being told fifteen.
10. **Margaret's portfolio recovers mid-catastrophe.** Flatlined at 60% loss at Hour 34 (06.1), at 47–51% and falling around Hour 43–44 (06.3).

### Minor

11. **Names.** Prose says **Damon Korr** throughout; every planning doc says **Voss** (the docs are stale, not the prose). Rosa **Santos** (Bangalore line 18) vs Rosa **Dela Cruz** (line 140). "Margaret **Chen**" for Kowalski (06.1 ~224). Maria Santos (São Paulo) collides with both.
12. **Arithmetic and materials (02/03).** Damon's "forty years" career at age 52; "quickite" is not a material (Roman concrete = pozzolana + quicklime + seawater); "one temporary death versus **a trillion** lives" vs ~10B humans; 9B vs 10.3B viewers.
13. **07/08 clocks.** "Five days in this chassis" vs the 3-day April 20→23 span; metadata "published at Hour 6" vs "propagated in sixteen hours"; backup cadence ("yesterday's checkpoint" vs weekly archiving); irreversibility acceleration to 49.7 at Hour 44 silently vanishes by Hour 48.
14. **Typos.** "Exactlys", "Your limited by", "something unease run through his body", "You didn't get the build the II's core algorithms right" (04); "a protocol change he didn't have predicted", "decided it the future of humanity" (03); "humanities' undivided attention" (02); leftover HTML comment at The Visit line 18; broken sentence at Resurrection line ~92.

## Recommended Fix Order

Ordered so no creative decision blocks a mechanical one. Passes 1–2 require zero inspiration — which is exactly what you want when stuck.

1. **Continuity spreadsheet pass** *(mechanical, 1–2 sessions)*. One table of clocks, hours, ages, prices, counts, names. Decide the master timeline (13 months, announcement April 15, 2050), pick 100K-or-48M, fix Korr/Voss in the planning docs, Chen/Kowalski, Soon-ok, the equity math. Raises S across six chapters without touching a single scene.
2. **De-glossing pass** *(mechanical, 1 session)*. Cut the narrator-decodes: the Divisadero coda (04), "None of them did" (01), the subtext essays (00), the "she didn't know" panorama (06.3), the line-209 gloss (07). Raises T everywhere. Pure deletion — the scenes already work.
3. **Rework The Resurrection** *(creative, the big one)*. Break the sentiment graph into fragmented, contradictory data — destruction and construction signals simultaneous, Myanmar bleeding red in the corner — and let Damon parse it silently. His silence in front of ambiguous data is scarier than his silence in front of a verdict.
4. **Arm The Confrontation** *(creative, contained)*. Weaponize the signature paradox and hand Damon the $45M argument he never makes. Two scenes, not a rewrite.
5. **Cascade consolidation — not restructure** *(editorial, 1–2 sessions)*. Cut 15–20% of beats: two of three Javi dashboard scenes, four of six "Log it" beats, the 06.2 recaps. Keep the intercut hinges. Optionally give Myanmar one ground-level face.
6. **Small strikes** *(as energy allows)*. Consolidate Ch 03's guilt loop to one descent; Seo-jin's consortium loss (still open); one sentence of Kaelen wondering whether Damon might have walked through the door himself (Fifteen Moves).

Then re-run the eval on the touched chapters and watch S and T climb toward the 4s that V already earns.
