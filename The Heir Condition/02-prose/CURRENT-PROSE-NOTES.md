## What The Current Draft Is Doing

The current prose is direct scene-based systems fiction, not documentary archive. The old media/family essaylet structure is superseded except as a tone bank. The prose now works best when technical explanation is carried by a person under pressure:

- Seo-jin thinking through care, nunchi, jeong, and hospital reimbursement while intubating patients.
- Rosa understanding patent licensing through the price of Lianne's GlucoSync.
- Javi watching a portfolio collapse and realizing his moral vocabulary was built by the market he served.
- Kaelen converting abstractions into named remainders.
- Damon recognizing his own architecture in the cascade and still finding the missing protocol.

## Current Strengths To Preserve

- **Embodied systems:** yakgwa, bark porridge, adobo, cold coffee, sandalwood, the new body's missing callus.
- **Taxonomies that matter:** three speeds of reality, formal vs. informal trust networks, explicit vs. implicit knowledge.
- **Compression after setup:** once Fifteen Moves renders a memory, later chapters should echo it rather than retell it.
- **Argument under emotional load:** the best dialogue happens when characters are both correct and compromised.
- **Korean/Tagalog texture:** gloss enough to orient the reader, then let some phrases carry meaning through context.
