## Shelf spine (exemplars, not the full library)

### Steal harder (Pauper primary)

- **Alastair Reynolds** — deep-time gothic, timeline weave, systems thinking. Pauper leans here over Weir.
- **Liu Cixin (Remembrance of Earth’s Past)** — civilizational pressure, game-theory knock-ons, bedrock upgrades that open into Dark Forest cosmic horror.
- **Isaac Asimov (Foundation)** — read as *psychohistory-in-the-making*: enough data and lessons to understand civilization now. Braided with Liu, not opposed to it.
- **Hugh Howey (Silo)** — sealed system, institutional lie, incremental bedrock upgrades into civilizational horror. Real steal (Liu/Reynolds lane).
- **Early Orson Scott Card / especially *Speaker for the Dead*** — cosmic mystery; human problems met with imperfect tradeoffs; moral weight. From Speaker specifically: **moral weight + tradeoffs** (not the full Card toolkit as default).

### Secondary (tactics, not spine)

- **Andy Weir** — competence / hard-SF needle-threading. Has a place; not the spine.
- **The Expanse** — mostly pleasure; usable secondary kit (crew/politics competence, slow cosmic threat). Steal tactics; don’t set the spine.

### Keep / kill reflexes

**KEEP**

- Cosmic / civilizational mystery that earns its horror from upgrades in understanding bedrock.
- Ugly tradeoffs; no “beautiful solutions where everything works.”
- Moral weight of hard understanding (Speaker), not of winning.
- Systems that fund space while people (jeong) fill it — Halmeoni’s Act 0 thesis as north star.
- Informal / human networks as load-bearing (gye, paluwagan, sujeonggwa on Tuesdays), not “noise below resolution.”
- Remainders ledgers; unverifiable price; Speed One (silicon) vs Speed Two (flesh / supply chain).
- Endings that leave the oculus open — incomplete men, cold second cup.

**KILL**

- Late-series rushed wrap-ups that tie mystery with a bow and tell you to stop thinking (*The Last Shadow* as exemplar).
- Absolving Kaelen; sealing the oculus; tidy uncle/nephew reconciliation.
- Amphitheater spectacle / nanobot POV tipping into competence porn.
- Contempt-for-the-masses thesis; woo; crank “exposé”; mystical shortcut to profundity; bad-faith certainty.
- **The 48-hour global sentiment uplift after Damon’s death** — hard kill. Chaos lasts months; in-window everything should trend down. Does not map to Speed One/Two mechanics. Uplift only when flesh/supply-chain/informal repair has had time to bite. (Last Shadow energy if kept.)
