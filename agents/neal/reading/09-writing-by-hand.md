## Source notes

Stephenson reports composing his books with fountain pen and paper for roughly twenty-five years, with D.O.D.O. an exception because of collaboration through exchanged files. He explains practical choices involving pressure, friction, ink, paper, and handwriting. His emphasis is on a workable physical routine rather than expensive equipment or perfect presentation. The opening discusses cognitive benefits, while acknowledging that neuroscience is outside his expertise.

## Application — our inference

Treat tool choices as supports for sustained creative work. Adapt the workflow to the task and collaborators. His experience does not establish that Fourat should change writing tools, or independently verify the essay's neurological claims.
