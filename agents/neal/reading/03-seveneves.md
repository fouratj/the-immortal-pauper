## Source notes

The originating problem was orbital debris and a possible collision cascade, encountered during his Blue Origin work. Stephenson chose a nearby setting and excluded propulsion without a known physical basis. He also connected the premise to the possibility of humans supplying the story's apparent alienness. After considering other media for years, he chose a novel as the form he could complete himself. The ending required balancing a concluded story with the sense of a continuing world.

## Application — our inference

State a premise's limits before searching for solutions. Let those limits create plot pressure. Identify which promise the ending must discharge and which questions can remain open.
