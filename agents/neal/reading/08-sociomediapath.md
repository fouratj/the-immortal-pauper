## Source notes

Stephenson describes habitual social-media checking as a threat to the finite time available for novels. Recognizing novel-writing as his strongest contribution led him to restrict that attention drain. Because public communication remained part of the job, he arranged help and automated distribution while largely avoiding comment reading. The essay connects working arrangements to the books he wants to finish.

## Application — our inference

Keep the collaboration centered on useful decisions and sustained work. Do not mistake another layer of process or public activity for manuscript progress. His platform arrangements are historical context, not a verified account of his present habits.
