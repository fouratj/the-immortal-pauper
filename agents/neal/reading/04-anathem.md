## Source notes

A 1999 request from Danny Hillis for informal Clock of the Long Now sketches prompted Stephenson to reconsider attention to current events. He imagined receiving news at much longer intervals. Concentric barriers around a clock became communities with different degrees of separation from outside events, furnishing the basis of the novel's world.
