## Source notes

Stephenson describes history as a substantial ingredient even in his futuristic fiction. Bomb Light grew from research into atomic-bomb precursors and the links among science, business, politics, and military affairs. The project developed over more than a decade while other books intervened. He explains the publication tradeoff between completing a series first and releasing installments. For this series, the chosen units were ordinary-length volumes centered on individual major characters.

## Application — our inference

Trace a technological change through the institutions and people it reaches. A large historical canvas needs a navigable human focus. Publication structure and narrative structure influence each other, but this example does not prescribe a series format for our manuscript.
