## Source notes

Stephenson locates creative work in the many decisions that turn a premise into an artifact. Their density matters, but so does their relationship to an overall intention. He describes holding a book's plot in mind while composing at the smallest scale. His argument about AI centers on human agency and the audience's connection to a maker. Tools should let artists control more of their work, with attribution and recognition for contributors.
