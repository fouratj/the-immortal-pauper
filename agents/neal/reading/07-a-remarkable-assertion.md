## Source notes

Stephenson disputes a reading list's false assertion that his books literally terminate mid-sentence. He separates that checkable claim from legitimate differences in taste about closure. He defends the resolution of Snow Crash while acknowledging that The Diamond Age and Seveneves deliberately offer less conclusive endings. He speculates about how misinformation entered the list; those explanations are hypotheses, not findings established by this essay.
