### What now works

**Damon's original achievement earns its place in the conflict.** Bangalore, line 202 onward, supplies a particular contract, excluded competent mechanics, an actual risk that required underwriting, a verifiable solution, and a successor business the original beneficiaries would have preferred to obstruct. The tender-rule change matters: technology does not magically repeal political permission. Kaelen's revised counterargument at line 248 accepts the achievement before identifying its later capture. This makes the nephew's action an application of his uncle's productive principle rather than a rejection of everything Damon built. The original book was much stronger at showing extraction than the liberation preceding it; this addition repairs that asymmetry.

**Bristol demonstrates a deliberately blocked alternative without making its audit omniscient.** Discontinuity, line 329 onward, separates cheaper individual cognition from a reorganized care business. Staffing, escalation, emergency burdens, transition costs, liability, and patient selection are addressed before the institutions withhold renewal. Those institutions have concrete interests and signatures. Their willingness to approve the scheduling component while preventing the competing organization makes the political mechanism legible. The passage explicitly does not answer Kenya: a limited successful trial is evidence against a particular prohibition, not proof that global simultaneous replacement is safe. Its location among failed remedies lets Kaelen interrogate evidence rather than interrupting the chapter with a second opening manifesto.

**The discovery argument prepares an outcome the protagonists cannot plan.** Fifteen Moves III, especially lines 82–90, grounds dispersed knowledge in humidity, shipment losses, and an operator's experience. Entry and exit, profit and loss, and the obligations that make exchange possible belong in the same explanation. The new Dr. Reyes exchange in Acceleration, lines 279–283, then turns that argument into work: competing manufacturers, an unanticipated packaging adaptation, failure data, money, and patient-facing approvals. Knowledge is available; finished safe production is not declared automatic. This pays off the digression rather than repeating its conclusion.

**Surrender finally has a serious temptation on the other side.** Cascade, lines 20–52, presents two available releases. Retaining approval and reduced royalties could preserve trials and prevent some dangerous uses. Kaelen's relinquishment therefore costs something he knows how to value. Keeping the trial casualties in the Remainders file prevents the choice from becoming moral certification. The earlier abdication scenario is remembered: the temptation to return after the next crisis has not vanished because he has named it. No invented permanent office or new planetary authority is necessary.

**Ownership dispersal now has operational content.** Cascade, line 143 onward, distinguishes operating companies from the common parent's restrictive control. Workers receive saleable shares, customers can move, interfaces open, and compulsory routing ends. Existing permits, power contracts, crews, payroll, and maintenance persist. The explicit limit on repealing national power rules keeps the energy/AI digressions compatible with the cascade. This is an opening in the capacity he owns, not instantaneous repeal of the world's political settlement.

The financial account is internally legible: $127 billion is an asset-and-reserve bundle; $7.35 billion funds 14,700 payments of $500,000; $119.65 billion is the remaining bundle at the former valuation, explicitly subject to decline. BioGenesis at line 309 onward transfers the estate's 67%, leaves minority ownership intact, releases controlled patents, and distinguishes the $20 billion employment/pension reserve from authority over future research. The projected competitive cost is not an imposed nonprofit price. The hundred-thousand immediate reach and forty-eight-million decade projection retain their different financing horizons.

**The ending tests the principle without closing history.** Confrontation line 175 refuses the inference that uncertain action makes waiting innocent. At line 245, an independent operator can refuse Kaelen priority, and he must ask others. Crucially, that is reconciliation research, not an operator arbitrarily stopping Myanmar's transfers. Damon's existing override remains necessary and limited; Kaelen's lost priority is neither a magical restoration nor abandonment. The cold second cup still has the final emotional word.

### Optional craft judgments, not required corrections

The expanded Fifteen Moves III is the most essay-like addition. It is defensible in a book whose intended pleasure includes sustained civilizational explanation, and its packaging example earns the length. If a later rhythm pass needs space, compress its repeated explanation of entry/exit before cutting the concrete example or removing the costs of lost research funding.

The final operator refusal is deliberately legible and slightly tidier than the surrounding emotional wreckage. I would retain it for this draft: it is short, places Kaelen under an ordinary consequence of his own decision, and ends before the untouched cup. Expanding it into a lesson or adding Washington's name would weaken it.

No additional digression, constitution, new surrender mechanism, or numerical-energy revision is needed to accept this batch.
