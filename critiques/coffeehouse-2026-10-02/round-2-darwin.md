**Darwin:** Mr. Hobbes asks what selects against an institution that keeps its promises to members by sacrificing outsiders. Nothing, if membership defines both observer and judge. Professor Ostrom offers monitoring by those who bear the consequences. But the child excluded from the savings circle and the sick patient a hospital does not recruit may bear consequences without appearing in its assembly. An appeal within the group could help it become a more efficient predator. How does the boundary itself change?

Let me carry the explanation beyond a ledger. Why does any society accumulate practices worth preserving? Usable energy and coordinated labor produce surplus above immediate survival. Surplus permits specialization: the nurse studies arrhythmia while somebody else grows food; a technician maintains the reactor while somebody else repairs the road. Specialization enlarges competence and dependence together. No one can inspect every act on which she relies. We inherit methods of checking: a clinical trial, a notary’s stamp, a neighborhood notebook. Each check consumes resources and creates an office. That office can improve trust, and because it controls a gate it can charge for entry. The toll funds the office; the office can fund the law making the toll obligatory. A practice selected because it enabled cooperation is now selected because it prevents replacement. That is Damon’s eleven-node graph. No funeral is needed to correct a measurement; some means of removing the monopoly over measurements is.

Mr. Hume is right that a replacement must earn belief in use. Yet habit can secure a bad practice. Rosa signs CarePath after Marco’s contract harmed him; she knows the danger but Lianne needs insulin in four days. Repetition shows constrained dependence, not confidence. At Hour 21, Ate Merced’s pool gets medicine to six families while formal channels reprice. If it becomes a compulsory distributor, as Damon’s Lagos evidence warns, it can replicate Eduardo’s stamp. Practical trust needs challenge from outside the trusted circle.

Professor Ostrom, your assembly and appeal are necessary, but how does the circle learn that a different keeper or public pharmacy is feasible? If members inspect Bae’s book but he controls their shifts and the only accessible pool, information may not become action. Name an outside option a worker can use. Mr. Hobbes, your guarantee may keep the ward open during reform. It may also preserve its position so completely that no rival can demonstrate a safer way to carry bad risks. What event ends the guarantee without handing that decision to its beneficiary?

Kenya is the severe counterexample to my account. Variation was tried. The best nurses left, patients who could not recover stayed, and Wanjiku lost a stable income. Selection happened rapidly and cruelly. I will not call that learning simply because someone later kept the post-mortem. A tolerable experiment must preserve the patient’s care while the financial form varies. That may require a pooled reserve, a rule against selecting only easy patients, and a real ability to halt the trial. Such safeguards are *necessary* for learning under humane conditions; they do not by themselves confer *legitimacy* on Damon’s unilateral control, prove *effectiveness* in a new corridor, or give his eventual choices *moral approval*. Those are separate judgments.

Now return to the chess lesson in *The Discontinuity*: Damon promises a system in which everyone has something, so no one has nothing to lose. Kaelen concludes that without desperation people will cease building mutual aid. I would have him challenge himself there. Soon-ok’s camp shared rice under mortal necessity; Seo-jin answers Dr. Reyes’s request for protocols amid a disrupted consortium, but the work calls upon curiosity, obligation, professional pride, and the hope that a treatment can travel. If a secure, well-fed society produced no competing clinics, no independent tests, and no new forms of aid, my account would fail inside this book. I suspect the danger is narrower and more interesting: people retain the impulse to vary, while Damon’s architecture controls which variations can persist. It selects permanence for the gatekeeper and calls the absence of visible competitors peace.
