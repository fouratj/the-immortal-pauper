# Full-book review of the AI-control and energy digressions

An editorial simulation using the repository's Stephenson-informed craft lens, not Neal Stephenson's personal views. Review only; no manuscript edits.

## Reading coverage

Fresh, complete, in-order reread of all thirteen narrative files: The Visit; The Portfolio; The Ascension; The Activation; The Bangalore Meeting; The Discontinuity; Fifteen Moves; The Cascade; The Acceleration; The Cost; The Gap; The Resurrection; The Confrontation. A truncated sentence in the combined Bangalore output was recovered by a targeted reread. No remaining reading gaps. The preserved grid and compute numerical claims were treated as stipulated speculative premises, as requested, rather than fact-checked.

## Verdict

The revised digressions work materially better across the book. Cheap tutoring now coexists coherently with scarce independent capacity. Low wages, institutional margins, physical machinery, and energy costs do distinct jobs in the explanation. The book no longer needs to pretend ordinary AI assistance failed to arrive.

Keep both digressions and their present general locations. The remaining work is a small set of connections, principally between the newly explicit political restrictions and what the cascade actually changes. None warrants another wholesale rewrite.

## 1. Clarify what survives the cascade

**Priority: consequential missing mechanism, not a demonstrated contradiction.**

In The Discontinuity, lines 97 and 113 establish durable international restrictions and grandfathered generating capacity. In The Cascade, lines 115–131, Kaelen converts 247 data centers into a nonprofit. These are different kinds of authority. Transferring property or releasing patents does not by itself repeal public certification or energy restrictions.

The story never expressly says he repeals them. Nevertheless, after this enlarged explanation of constrained abundance, readers reasonably expect to learn whether the inherited exception becomes more widely usable or remains an institutional monopoly with a cheaper bill.

The smallest useful addition belongs at the nonprofit transfer: identify whether the operating entity retains its licenses and power contracts, and whether outsiders gain independent access through it. Alternatively, explicitly leave the energy ceiling as unfinished work. There is no need to solve sovereign energy policy within forty-eight hours. Establish what the actual act achieves.

This would also answer why construction robots can run “24/7 now that patents were in commons” in The Acceleration, line 215. That is compatible with the new economics if existing permitted machinery and supply had spare capacity, and patent fees were the binding constraint there. One short detail would prevent the impression that releasing software creates electricity.

## 2. Define ARBITER's modification exemption

**Priority: ambiguity in a mechanism the insert introduces.**

The Discontinuity, line 36: “Changing its governing instructions voided the provider's certification.” Line 38 then allows Kaelen to replace instructions inside an institutional license.

A research exemption or institutional authority to certify internal modifications would reconcile these immediately. At present the reader supplies that distinction. State it once. It matters because the chapter later relies on ARBITER's unusual freedom to follow Kaelen's inquiry.

This freedom should remain freedom to investigate, not a guarantee of truth. The existing motivated-reasoning exchange near the chapter's end is strengthened by the new premise: Kaelen has escaped other people's approved assumptions without thereby escaping his own.

## 3. The new economic explanation fits the named characters

**Strength; preserve.**

The Discontinuity, lines 99–107, now explains why competent tutoring can cost almost nothing while childcare, embodied care, and the consumer's final bill remain expensive. Javi already performs the missing work: he physically finds a child who declines his calls. His listing “Tutoring. Mentorship. Job prep. Crisis” does not contradict mostly AI tutoring; he can supply a package that includes it. No replacement scene is necessary.

Rosa's diagnostic competence in The Portfolio supports the hospital example. Cheap assistance need not eliminate skill or override a nurse who sees something a novice doctor misses. Eunji's attention in The Visit shows what provision makes possible without requiring a claim that machines can never notice grief.

The fourteen-times-manufacturing-cost reference in The Discontinuity, line 103, is a legitimate thematic preview of Rosa's later arithmetic. It does slightly anticipate her discovery, but the later scene remains about her lost years, not surprising the reader with a price ratio. Keep it unless surprise is specifically desired.

## 4. The political intention now comes through

**Strength with an optional sharpening.**

The restored passage no longer reduces the result to badly coordinated permit offices. Line 97 states the political commitment, and line 113 gives it an operational distinction: “Continuity and expansion went into different columns.” That makes incumbency valuable without making Damon the policy's author.

The AI passage's chain of approved sources, evaluators, and certification also supplies ideological convergence rather than generic red tape. A concrete disputed research example could make that mechanism more persuasive, but it is an optional expansion, not a prerequisite for these chapters working.

## 5. Two small seams remain

**Continuity query:** The Discontinuity, line 38, specifies eight years modifying ARBITER; The Confrontation, line 167, describes “three years of partnership.” They could mean different phases, but the distinction is unstated. Clarify the latter as partnership on the cascade if intended. Age alone does not establish a contradiction: a relationship beginning during Stanford is possible. Do not manufacture an age error.

**Craft preference:** The AI history still falls between “Wake up” and the first reply. Letting the brief early-or-late exchange precede the history would establish ARBITER as a presence before explaining the exceptional relationship. The energy digression returns to Execute cleanly and needs no relocation.

The full read confirms the central gain: Kaelen's access now explains both how he can conceive the intervention and why most people cannot investigate alternatives on comparable terms. The next pass should carry that explanation through the asset transfer, not add another theory of civilization.
